# Jurisio ⚖️  
AI-Powered Legal Document Simplifier

## 📌 Overview

Jurisio is a web-based application that transforms complex legal documents into clear, simplified language for non-lawyers. It helps users understand contracts, policies, agreements, and legal notices without requiring formal legal training.

The goal is to bridge the gap between legal complexity and everyday comprehension using AI-powered text simplification and structured explanations.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📄 Upload or paste legal documents
- ✂️ Section-wise simplification
- 🧠 AI-powered rewriting into plain language
- 📚 Context-aware explanations of legal terms
- 🪞 Side-by-side view (Original vs Simplified)
- 🔍 Improved readability for general users

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Legal documents are often filled with complex terminology, long sentences, and ambiguous clauses. Most people sign agreements without fully understanding them.

Jurisio simplifies legal text into readable, structured explanations, empowering users to make informed decisions.

---

## 🧩 How It Works

1. User uploads or pastes legal text.
2. The system processes the content using AI-based rewriting.
3. The text is simplified into plain language.
4. Key sections are explained in an easy-to-understand format.
5. Users can compare original and simplified versions side-by-side.
